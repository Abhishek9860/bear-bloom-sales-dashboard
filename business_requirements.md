# Bear & Bloom — Business Requirements

## Business Overview
Bear & Bloom is a fictional e-commerce retailer used for this portfolio project. The analysis uses the Maven Analytics e-commerce dataset.

## Business Objective
Analyze sales performance, profitability, customer purchasing behavior, and marketing effectiveness to identify opportunities for business growth.

## Key Business Questions
1. How are revenue, orders, and gross profit changing over time?
2. Which products contribute most to revenue and gross profit?
3. How do customer purchasing patterns change over time?
4. Which marketing sources and campaigns generate orders?
5. How do website sessions convert into purchases?

## Deliverables
- Interactive Power BI dashboard
- Documented DAX measures
- Data model and data dictionary
- Business insights and recommendations
- GitHub portfolio repository
